Pengu app alternatives: shared pets for couples and friends

Updated November 6, 2026 · 7 min read · How we write these

Short answer

Pengu is now listed on the App Store as Friends: Pengu, Bao & Mellow, an AI companion app with ads and an optional Pengu Pass. For another shared pet, Widgetable and Pookie run on home-screen widgets, FurTwo splits care duties between two partners, and SumOne grows an egg from a daily question. For something that needs no tapping, Pikmin Bloom pools Apple Health steps in weekly challenges, and Apple's Fitness app lets two people share rings and compete for 7 days, free.

You and your partner raised a penguin together for months. Then the ads got louder, the outfits moved behind the Pengu Pass, and the app started calling itself Friends. You still want a small shared thing on both your phones. Just maybe not this one.

What Pengu is now (and why people leave)

Pengu still exists. On the US App Store it is listed as Friends: Pengu, Bao & Mellow, by SLAY GmbH. Pengu is described as a shared AI companion you raise with a partner or close friend. Bao is a solo companion for self care and habits, and Mellow is one for studying and focus. You send reactions and gifts, dress the companions up, decorate rooms and watch them on a home-screen widget. It needs iOS 17 or later.

The business model is the reason most people go looking. The app is free to download, the listing says it contains ads, and the paid layer is the Pengu Pass, with separate partner tiers. Prices on the listing have changed often and vary by country, so check the figure inside the app.

Read the recent App Store reviews and the same complaints keep coming back:

  • Ads. Before games, on launch, and on daily rewards.
  • The Pass. Reviewers say clothing, items and mission skips sit behind it.
  • Bugs. Crashes, frozen pets and lost coins, reported across 2024 to 2026.
  • The AI turn. Some long-time players did not sign up for a chat companion, they signed up for a penguin.

Those are reviews, not a verdict, and plenty of couples still enjoy it. But if any of those is your reason, the alternatives below sort themselves by which problem they fix.

The free answer: what Apple already lets two people share

Apple has no shared pet. What it does have is a free, ad-free way to have one small thing you both check every day.

Fitness sharing. Open the Fitness app on iPhone, tap Sharing, then Invite a Friend. On Apple Watch, open the Activity app and tap Invite a Friend. You each see the other's Activity rings for the last 7 days, plus finished workouts. To make it a game, tap their name and choose Compete: you earn a point for every percent of your rings you close, up to 600 a day, for 7 days. Your watch tells you when you pull ahead.

Health Sharing. For more detail, open Health, tap Sharing, then Share with Someone, and pick topics such as sleep or activity. It goes one way, so each of you sets it up. It is real data, not a cute summary, so check the topic list first. We walk through both in sleep tracking with a partner or friends.

What this misses is the thing Pengu got right: a creature. Rings do not wave at you. That is the gap the apps below fill.

Widgetable and Pookie: closest to the Pengu feel

Widgetable is the big one: you adopt a pet with a friend or partner, feed and play with it, and it lives on your home screen next to a sleep widget, a distance widget and shared notes. It is also the most Pengu-like in money. Reviewers complain that free food is gated behind ads and the rest is sold, and the listing has sold ad-free days as a separate purchase.

Pookie is newer and calmer. You adopt one pet together, feed, water and play with it, and the latest updates move pets into a shared garden. Levelling up pays out hearts that unlock new pets and decorations. The detail that matters for couples: one Premium subscription covers both partners.

FurTwo: a pet that needs both of you

FurTwo takes the "together" part literally. One partner handles play, training and sleep. The other handles feeding, hygiene and healing. Hunger, happiness, energy and health are tracked stats, and the listing mentions memorial pets you can revive, so neglect has a cost. That is great when you live together and fun when you are both in it. It is harder when one of you goes quiet for a week.

SumOne: a pet that grows from talking

SumOne is a question app first. Every day you both get one question, and your answers hatch and grow an egg called EggMon. You can share a mood with each answer. If what you actually liked about Pengu was the excuse to check in with each other, this is the most direct version. Reviewers mention feeding EggMon repeatedly for pebbles, so it is not free of grind.

Finch and Pikmin Bloom: not a shared pet, still together

Finch gives each of you your own bird. You add each other with a friend code from the Friends tab and send free Good Vibes. Your bird runs on goals you each log. We compare it with gentler options in self-care tamagotchi apps, and with grown-up ones in Finch alternatives for adults.

Pikmin Bloom is the one you never have to remember. Steps come from Apple Health, and its Weekly Challenges let you team up with people anywhere toward a shared step goal. Niantic's help center says syncing can lag by several hours, and it needs Steps turned on for Pikmin Bloom in Health's data access settings.

Long distance: pick by effort and time zones

For a long distance relationship, the art matters less than how the app handles two people who are rarely awake at the same time.

  • Asynchronous is easier. Widget pets (Widgetable, Pookie, Friends) let each of you drop in whenever. Nothing waits on the other person.
  • Split duties raise the stakes. FurTwo's design means one person's busy week shows up as the pet's bad week. Some couples love that pressure. Others end up arguing about a cartoon dog.
  • A daily ritual suits some couples. SumOne's one question a day fits a goodnight call, whatever the time difference.
  • Zero effort is an option. Steps in Pikmin Bloom and rings in Apple Fitness happen whether you open anything or not.

One more check before you both install: who pays. Pookie's listing says one Premium subscription covers both of you. For the others, read the in-app purchase list on the listing before either of you subscribes.

If you would rather the pet ran on something real, like sleep or steps, see virtual pet apps that use Apple Health. For sleep specifically, gamified sleep tracker apps covers Pokémon Sleep, which lets friends see each other's sleep length and type.

Where Nularo fits

Full disclosure: Nularo is our app, and it is not a Pengu clone. There is no shared pet to feed and nothing to tap.

Each of you gets your own creature. It reads Apple Health read-only (sleep, steps, active minutes, workouts, resting heart rate, HRV and more) and every morning lands in one of four moods: glowing, fine-but-judgy, wrecked or suspicious. Each creature measures its owner against their own 14-day baseline, so a six-hour sleeper and an eight-hour sleeper are both judged fairly.

Friend mode is the "together" part. The two creatures sit side by side, and your partner sees your mood word and good-day count. They never see your sleep, steps or heart numbers. Across a time difference that works well, because nobody has to be awake for the other's creature to update. The creature judges, it does not cheer. If you like that tone, more apps that roast you exist.

The honest limitation: it is iPhone only, so an Android partner is out, and your partner needs it on their own iPhone. Without an Apple Watch the sleep and heart data is thin, and the verdict lands the morning after, not in real time. It is $39.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, or $9.99 a month, with a hard paywall after onboarding.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pengu still on the App Store?

Yes, under a new name. The app is listed as Friends: Pengu, Bao & Mellow by SLAY GmbH. Pengu is still the companion you raise with a partner or friend, and Bao and Mellow are solo companions for self care and studying. It is free to download, contains ads and offers an optional Pengu Pass.

What is the best Pengu alternative for long distance couples?

One you can both play at different times. Widgetable and Pookie work through home-screen widgets, so each of you checks in when it suits you. FurTwo gives each partner a different set of care jobs, which is more involved but harder across time zones. SumOne needs one answer a day each.

Is there a free couples pet app without ads?

Not among the big ones we checked. Widgetable and Friends (Pengu) list ads, SumOne reviewers describe ad-based rewards, and sell in-app purchases, and Pookie and FurTwo sell premium plans. The free, ad-free way to share something daily is Apple's own Fitness sharing, which shows each other's Activity rings and runs 7-day competitions.

Can two people share a pet that runs on health data?

Not one pet, as far as we could find. Pikmin Bloom lets friends pool Apple Health steps toward a weekly goal. Nularo gives each person their own creature fed by Apple Health, and friend mode puts the two side by side, sharing only a mood word and a good-day count.
