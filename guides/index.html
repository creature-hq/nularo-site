Recovery, stress and HRV

HRV, resting heart rate, stress and readiness-style scores on Apple Watch, and what the numbers mean.

  • Whoop alternatives for Apple Watch: get the scores, skip the strapYour Apple Watch already records what Whoop scores. Readiness (Series 12), Athlytic, Bevel, Training Today and Nularo turn it into recovery for less.
  • Does Apple Watch resting heart rate include sleep?No. Apple Watch resting heart rate leaves out readings taken while you sleep, so it runs higher than the overnight number Oura, Whoop or Garmin show.
  • Does Apple Watch track HRV during sleep? How to see your night HRVYes, but most watches take only a few HRV readings a night. Series 12 and Ultra 4 sample far more often and show overnight HRV in Vitals.
  • Does Apple Watch have a recovery score? Three things called recoveryOnly Series 12 and Ultra 4 get one: Readiness, a 0-10 score. Every watch since watchOS 9 has Cardio Recovery, which is a heart rate drop, not a score.
  • Free stress apps for Apple Watch: what they actually measureApple Watch has no stress meter. Bevel and Athlytic add a free stress monitor from heart rate and HRV. Welltory gives one reading a day.
  • Why is my Apple Watch HRV always low? (What the number means)Apple Watch reports HRV as SDNN from spot readings, so it is not on the same scale as Whoop or Oura. Compare it with your own trend, not other people's.
  • Does Apple Watch have a Body Battery? (The closest equivalents)No. Body Battery is Garmin's. Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 get a 0-10 Readiness score, and apps like Bevel and Athlytic add a battery-style gauge.
