What Nularo does

Nularo reads sleep, movement, and heart information from Apple Health with your permission, displays it, and gives you one reaction each morning in the voice of a small creature that lives in the app. It also calculates a "Creature Age" from your habits, using published public health guidelines as a reference. That number is a rules-based interpretation of behavior. It is not a clinical measurement, not a biological age, and not a diagnosis.

The creature's voice

The creature is blunt on purpose. It is written to be funny and unimpressed, and it reacts to what you did rather than telling you what to do. It is a character. If you would rather it were gentler, you can change its savagery in Settings, and you can stop using it whenever you like.

AI-generated content

The creature's daily line is written by an AI model, not a person, and nobody reviews it before it reaches you. We run it through an automated check first, one that looks for the things this character must never say (advice, diagnosis, anything about body or weight) and swaps in a line we wrote ourselves if it fails, but that check is a set of rules, not a human editor. Treat every line as entertainment, written in character. It can still be wrong, oddly phrased, or miss the point of your night, the same as any other automated system can.

The model is Google's Gemini, and it only ever runs if you say yes. The first time Nularo asks to read Apple Health, it also asks, separately, whether you want your verdict written by AI. That question is off until you turn it on. If you leave it off, or switch it off later under Settings, nothing about your nights is sent to Google and the creature writes from lines we wrote by hand. Exactly what gets sent, and what does not, is listed in our Privacy Policy.
