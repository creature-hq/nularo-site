What Nularo does

Reads Apple Health for you

Sleep, sleep stages, steps, workouts, resting heart rate and HRV come in automatically, read-only. You never log anything.

A verdict every morning

One honest notification about last night. The creature wakes up glowing, fine-but-judgy, wrecked or suspicious, measured against your own 14-day baseline.

Creature Age

Every week your sleep, bedtime consistency, steps, active minutes, resting heart rate and HRV become an age, with a breakdown of which habit added the years.

Friends, minus the oversharing

Friend mode shows a mood word and a good-day count. Never your health numbers.
